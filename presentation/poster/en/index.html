Albus

A little blockchain I built for fun.

Hi, I'm Gilbert, and I'm a student. Albus is my hobby project: I really wanted to understand how a blockchain works, so I just built one myself. With a node, a wallet, an explorer and even a mining program.

I'm not trying to make money with it, and I have no plan to. It's simply fun, and I learned a huge amount along the way. This page explains how it all works.

A shared notebook

Picture a notebook where everyone writes down who gave what to whom. And everybody has a copy. If you wanted to cheat, you'd have to erase the same line in every copy at the same time.

The notebook is made of blocks. Each block contains the hash (think of it as a fingerprint) of the block before it. If someone changes something later, the fingerprint no longer matches and the chain breaks. No bank needed.

41Anna to Ben 5hash 0000c92e…
42Ben to Cleo 2hash 0000a3f1…
43Cleo to Anna 1hash 00007de0…

Each block points to the one before it by its hash. Change one and the chain breaks.

Where new blocks come from: mining

A miner keeps trying numbers (nonces) until the block's SHA-256 hash starts with enough zeros. Searching is hard work, checking is instant. Whoever finds a block gets 10 ALB.

Every node checks new blocks for itself. If there are two versions of the chain, the one with the most work in it wins. So you don't have to trust anybody.
