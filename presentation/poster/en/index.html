Albus

A little blockchain I built for fun.

Hi, I'm Gilbert, and I'm a student. Albus is my hobby project: I really wanted to understand how a blockchain works, so I just built one myself. With a node, a wallet, an explorer and even a mining program.

I'm not trying to make money with it, and I have no plan to. It's simply fun, and I learned a huge amount along the way. This page explains how it all works.

Start mining in 3 minutes

Download the Albus miner

Free app for Windows. It sets everything up, mines on your own PC and updates itself.

⬇ Download for Windows
  1. Click the big button. A small file install-albus.ps1 is saved.
  2. Right-click it and choose Run with PowerShell.
  3. The miner window opens in your browser. Create your wallet there.

Faster: paste this one line into PowerShell instead.

irm https://albus.workflowsolved.com/releases/install-albus.ps1 | iex
  • You need Node.js and PowerShell 7. The installer offers to get them for you.
  • Every update is signed by the Albus publisher and checked on your PC. Your wallet and keys never leave it.
  • The program is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a warning. Read the script first if you like, it is short.

Your own servers and farm

More computers, one wallet: your server or any extra PC mines for your address. They do not need the private key, it stays on your main PC. Every computer shows up with name and speed in the miner window.

  1. Copy your address (albus1…) from the miner window.
  2. On the server or PC paste one of the two commands. Replace albus1DEINE_ADRESSE with your address.
  3. After a few seconds the computer appears in the miner under Your farm and servers.

Linux server (as root, with PowerShell 7):

curl -fsSLO https://albus.workflowsolved.com/releases/install-farm.ps1 && sudo pwsh -File install-farm.ps1 -Address albus1DEINE_ADRESSE -Name Server-1

Windows PC (PowerShell 7 as administrator):

irm https://albus.workflowsolved.com/releases/install-farm.ps1 -OutFile install-farm.ps1; ./install-farm.ps1 -Address albus1DEINE_ADRESSE -Name Rig-1

⬇ Download the farm script

  • Every computer needs Node.js 20 or newer. Linux: processor (CPU) only, no graphics card.
  • The computer then runs as a service and starts by itself after every reboot. To remove it: run the same script with -Uninstall.

A shared notebook

Picture a notebook where everyone writes down who gave what to whom. And everybody has a copy. If you wanted to cheat, you'd have to erase the same line in every copy at the same time.

The notebook is made of blocks. Each block contains the hash (think of it as a fingerprint) of the block before it. If someone changes something later, the fingerprint no longer matches and the chain breaks. No bank needed.

41Anna to Ben 5hash 0000c92e…
42Ben to Cleo 2hash 0000a3f1…
43Cleo to Anna 1hash 00007de0…

Each block points to the one before it by its hash. Change one and the chain breaks.

Where new blocks come from: mining

A miner keeps trying numbers (nonces) until the block's SHA-256 hash starts with enough zeros. Searching is hard work, checking is instant. Whoever finds a block gets 10 ALB.

Every node checks new blocks for itself. If there are two versions of the chain, the one with the most work in it wins. So you don't have to trust anybody.
